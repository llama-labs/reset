Good to know

Questions, answered.

What exactly is RESET?

A full day out of the city with a small group: games, team challenges, movement and facilitated sessions, a long lunch together and time to slow down. Think adult playground meets a day in nature.

Can I come alone?

Yes, and most people do. That’s kind of the point: you’ll leave knowing a lot more people than you arrived with.

Do I need to be fit?

Not really. Everything is designed so anyone can join in, at their own pace. If you’re up for moving and laughing, you’re good.

Is this a workout?

Not really. You might break a sweat, but it’s definitely not a bootcamp. Play comes first.

Will my phone be taken away?

Yes, in the nicest way. There’s a little phone handover ceremony when you arrive, and you get it back at the end of the day. A proper day to disconnect.

What should I bring?

Trainers, comfortable clothes you can move in, a few layers (it’s November) and a water bottle. We’ll take care of the rest.

How do I get there?

Quinta da Pedra Firme is in Colares, about 50 km from Lisbon and easy by Uber. Before the day we’ll set up a WhatsApp group so you can share rides with other guests.

What if it rains?

In case of bad weather, the event will be cancelled and everyone gets a full refund.

Why are we doing this?

City life can be intense. We believe the truest medicine for our nervous system is simple: switching off, being in good company and spending time in nature. We want more of that in our lives, and in yours. This is our first edition, and we’re really excited. If it works, we’d love to run a few RESET days throughout next year.
